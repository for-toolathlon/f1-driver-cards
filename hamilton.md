| Position | Points |

Born on 7 January 1985 in Stevenage, Hamilton turned professional in 2003 with McLaren, co-won the 2008 championship, moved to Mercedes in 2013 and finished 8 consecutive seasons in the top five before that headline 2024, finishing world number two with 222 points and a historic fourth-again-with-Carlsberg line.
