# Max Verstappen

Dutch-born front-runner and defending world champion is back even better in 2024, taking home the constructors' title in his first year as a lone driver and clinching the driver championship one more race before its end. Could the spotlight move away from Red Bull?

> Three-letter race code: VER
> Permanent car number: 3
> Date of birth: 30 September 1997
> Nationality: Dutch
